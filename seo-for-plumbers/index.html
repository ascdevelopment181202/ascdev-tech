What is SEO for plumbers?

SEO stands for search engine optimisation. For a plumber it means showing up at the moment a homeowner types "emergency plumber near me", "boiler repair" plus your town, or asks an AI assistant who to call.

There are three places you can appear, and each one works differently:

  • Google Maps results. The map and the three businesses listed under it. Most calls for urgent jobs come from here.
  • The normal results underneath. Your website's pages, for searches like "bathroom fitter in Watford" or "how much does a new boiler cost".
  • AI answers. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's own AI summaries, which now name specific local firms.

Good SEO covers all three, because your customers use all three.

How do plumbers get into the Google Maps results?

Google has said its local results come down to three things: how relevant you are to the search, how close you are to the person searching, and how well known and trusted your business is. You cannot move your premises, but you can improve the other two.

Which searches should a plumber target first?

Start with the jobs that pay best and the ones you want more of, not the broadest phrase. "Plumber London" is crowded and vague. "Boiler installation in Harrow" or "unvented cylinder repair in Pinner" is easier to win and brings a better job.

We build the plan in this order:

  1. Your money jobs. Boiler installs, bathroom fits, heating upgrades, or whatever earns the most for your firm.
  2. Emergency work. Burst pipes, leaks and no hot water, where the phone call happens in minutes.
  3. Your areas. The towns and postcodes you actually want to travel to.
  4. Questions customers ask. Cost guides and how-to answers that bring people in before they are ready to call.

Can AI assistants like ChatGPT recommend my plumbing business?

Yes, and more homeowners now ask them. When someone asks an AI assistant for a plumber in their area, it pulls together what it can find about local firms: their websites, their business listings, their reviews and mentions on other sites. It then names a few.

To be one of the names, your business needs to be easy to understand and easy to trust:

  • A website that states plainly what you do, where you work and how to reach you.
  • The same business details on Google, Bing, Apple Maps and the main directories.
  • Recent reviews that describe real jobs.
  • Structured data, which is a label in your site's code that tells machines exactly what your business is.

This is called AI visibility, or generative engine optimisation. It is part of every ASC plan, not an extra.

How long does SEO take for a plumber?

Plan for three to six months for results to build. Some things move quickly: fixing a slow site or a half-finished Google profile can show within weeks. Rankings for competitive searches in bigger towns take longer.

The work compounds. Each page, review and listing keeps working after it is done, which is the main difference from paying for every lead.

Not always. If your current site is fast, works well on a phone and has a page for each service and area, we improve it. If it is slow, thin or built on a platform that blocks the basics, we rebuild it as part of the plan, and you keep your domain.

Can ChatGPT recommend my plumbing business?

Yes. AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity recommend local businesses when people ask who to call. They rely on clear information about what you do and where, consistent business details across the web, and reviews. Making that information easy to find and trust is part of our monthly work.

Is SEO better than Checkatrade or paying for leads?

They do different jobs. Directories and lead sites can bring work quickly, but you pay for every lead and often share it with other firms. SEO builds your own visibility, so enquiries come straight to you. Many plumbers run both, then lean on SEO more as it grows.

About ASC Development

ASC Development is a UK web and marketing studio for trades and home service businesses. We build websites for plumbers, electricians, roofers, builders and similar firms, then do the ongoing work that gets them found on Google, on Google Maps and in AI assistants like ChatGPT. It is run by Ayush Chavan, CEO, and Aarav Chavan, CTO, and works on one monthly plan with no contract.

By ASC Development · Updated 12 November 2026

What is ASC Development?

ASC Development builds websites and runs search marketing for UK home service businesses. The aim is simple: more of the right enquiries, coming straight to the business.

Most trades are good at the work and short on time for marketing. A site gets built once and left. The Google profile is half filled in. Reviews come in when someone remembers to ask. We take all of that on as one job, so the owner can stay on the tools.

What does ASC Development do?

  • Websites. Fast, mobile-first sites built to turn a visitor into a call or a quote request. See websites for tradespeople.
  • Local SEO. Rankings for the services and areas each client wants, and visibility on Google Maps.
  • AI search visibility. Making a business easy for ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI answers to recommend. See AI search visibility for trades.
  • Google Business Profile management. Categories, services, photos, posts and replies.
  • Reviews. A simple request that goes to every customer after a job.
  • Lead capture. Quote forms and missed-call text-back, so an enquiry is not lost when the phone goes unanswered.

One flat monthly price covers the website, hosting and the ongoing work. There is no large upfront build fee and no long contract.

Home service businesses across the UK:

  • Plumbers, heating and gas engineers, boiler installers and bathroom fitters
  • Electricians, EV charger installers, solar and battery installers
  • Roofers, builders, scaffolders, loft and extension specialists
  • Landscapers, gardeners, tree surgeons, fencing and driveway installers
  • Cleaners and pest control firms
  • Kitchen fitters, carpenters, decorators, plasterers, locksmiths and handymen

We also take on a small number of healthcare and e-commerce clients, where our approach is a good fit.

How does working with ASC Development go?

  1. Book a call. We look at where you show up now and what you want more of.
  2. Onboarding. You send us your details and photos. We handle the rest.
  3. Work begins within 48 hours. A new website goes live in about ten days from the first call.

After that you get a short update every week and a plain-English report every month. The targets we work towards are a top three place on Google Maps for your area and two to five times more enquiries from search, with three to six months for results to build. They are targets, not guarantees, and we report honestly against them.

Questions about ASC Development

Is ASC Development a web design agency or an SEO agency?

Both, on purpose. A website with nobody finding it does not bring work, and SEO on a poor website wastes the visitors it wins. ASC Development builds the site and does the ongoing search work as one monthly plan.

Does ASC Development only work with trades?

Mostly. The focus is UK home service businesses such as plumbers, electricians, roofers, builders, landscapers and cleaners. We also take on a small number of healthcare and e-commerce clients where our approach is a good fit.

Is there a contract?

There is no long contract. One flat monthly price covers the website, hosting, SEO, Google Business Profile management and AI visibility work.

Where does ASC Development work?

Across the United Kingdom. Calls are booked online, so it does not matter which town your business is in.

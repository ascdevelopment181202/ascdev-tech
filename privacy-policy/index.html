Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information ASC Development collects through www.ascdev.tech, why, who it is shared with and what your rights are. The short version: the website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, and we only hold the details you choose to send us.

ASC Development · Updated 28 November 2026

Who is responsible for your information?

ASC Development is the trading name of Ayush Chavan, a sole trader based in the United Kingdom. Ayush Chavan is the data controller for the information described here.

To ask a question or use any of your rights, email chavanayush802@gmail.com or call or message 07784 071383.

What information do we collect?

  • Enquiry details. The form on our homepage asks for your first name, phone number, email address, what you need, your location and a message. The form does not send these to our website. It opens WhatsApp with your answers filled in as a message to us, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.
  • Call bookings. If you book a call through our Google Calendar booking page, we receive the details you enter on that page, such as your name and email address.
  • Messages and calls. If you phone, text, message on WhatsApp or email us, we hold your contact details and what you tell us.
  • Client information. If you become a client, we hold the business and contact details needed to do the work and to invoice you.

Does the website use cookies or tracking?

No. The pages on www.ascdev.tech do not set cookies, and we do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts on them.

Loading any website involves some technical data. Our pages are hosted by Vercel, and they load fonts from Google Fonts and one script from the jsDelivr network. When your browser asks for those files, those providers receive your IP address and basic browser details, as with any web request. We do not use that data to identify you.

Why do we use your information, and on what legal basis?

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call. You asked us to, and it is in our legitimate interests to respond to people who contact us.
  • To provide our services to clients. This is needed to carry out our contract with you.
  • To keep business and tax records. This is needed to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your information. We do not add you to a marketing list because you sent an enquiry.

Who do we share it with?

Only the services we use to run the business, and only as far as they need it:

  • WhatsApp, for enquiry messages.
  • Google, for the call booking page, calendar and email.
  • Vercel, which hosts the website.

These providers may store or process information outside the United Kingdom. Each has its own privacy policy that explains how it handles data. We may also share information where the law requires us to.

How long do we keep it?

We keep enquiry messages and contact details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows. If you become a client, we keep records for as long as the law requires for business and tax purposes. You can ask us to delete your details at any time, and we will unless the law requires us to keep them.

What are your rights?

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • limit or stop how we use it;
  • pass it to you or another provider in a usable format.

Contact us using the details above and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Will this policy change?

We will update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. See also our Terms of Use.
